# `meshcore-local` fixtures

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `companion-tcp.hex` | **`synthetic: true`.** What a MeshCore WiFi companion sends on its TCP port during the startup sequence and afterwards, radio to app: each frame is `>` (0x3E), a uint16 little-endian length and the frame, as hex after a `#` label. Self info, device info, the eight channel slots (`Public`, `Ops`, six empty; the secrets are present, as the firmware sends them, and must never be read), a contact list of three (chat, a repeater whose last advert is 14 h old, a room), the end of contacts, battery, no more messages, then a `PUSH_CODE_ADVERT` (key only) for the repeater and the contact frame that answers its re-query, a `PUSH_CODE_NEW_ADVERT` for a new sensor, `PUSH_CODE_MSG_WAITING`, a channel message (V3) on slot 1, a direct message (V3), no more messages; then an oversize frame (length 200) and two junk bytes before a last frame. |
| `commands.hex` | **`synthetic: true`.** The commands Foresignal writes for that stream, app to radio (`<` envelope): app start ("Foresignal"), device query 3, get channel 0–7, get contacts, battery, sync next message, contact by key, and the sync loop. |

Keys are SHA-256 of synthetic strings (`synthetic-own`, `synthetic-hiker`, `synthetic-repeater`, `synthetic-room`,
`synthetic-sensor`), so node ids are `mc:f7ff142f1f28` (companion), `mc:7b065359ae9c` (hiker), `mc:5f6a9ca3f1cc`
(repeater), `mc:d104384f64d9` (room) and `mc:74979e2bf73d` (sensor). Times are around 1791900000 (2026-10-13).

Layouts follow the companion firmware (`examples/companion_radio/MyMesh.cpp`, `writeContactRespFrame`, and
`src/helpers/esp32/SerialWifiInterface.cpp` for the TCP envelope) and `docs/companion_protocol.md` in the MIT MeshCore
repository, read from the local reference clone. No companion was reachable during the build; the stream was generated by
a short script with those layouts.

**Recapture** (prompt 143, on the operator's hardware): a passive capture cannot show the startup answers, because the
companion only answers commands. Enable `meshcore-local` against the companion with `logging.level` of
`ai.foresignal.source.adapters.mesh` at `DEBUG`, or capture with `tcpdump -w companion.pcap host <companion> and port
<port>` while the source starts, and export both directions as hex.
