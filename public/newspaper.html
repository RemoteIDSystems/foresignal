THE PUBLISHING PIPELINE

One scheduled workflow.
A paper built for you.

News intake and the edition engine already live inside the Foresignal backend. The Admin app exposes operational configuration, while authorized users can manage their own editions.

01

Follow what matters

Choose news outlets, languages and the wider intelligence scope: places, sources, alert rules, and saved views.

02

Translate and organize

Group articles into stories, distinguish independent reporting, and translate foreign-language titles and excerpts.

03

Write and narrate

Generate a cited AI narrative or deterministic digest, with optional speech synthesis in the edition's selected language.

04

Publish and deliver

Reach the reader through email, push summaries, hosted pages, alerts and podcast feeds. Trigger breaking editions from watched alerts.

EDITORIAL ACCOUNTABILITY BUILT IN

Know why it's in your paper.

Foresignal's edition engine can attach evidence provenance, independent-source counts and corroboration assessments to cited items. It can distinguish reporting breadth from the strength of the underlying evidence.

  • Record references and reasons for inclusion
  • Independent evidence counted without wire-copy inflation
  • Ratings imported from identified providers, never invented
  • Signed stored editions and audio
