THINK ACROSS TIME, NOT JUST FEEDS

From raw signals to
defensible understanding.

01

Remember the evolution

Store meaningful changes in versioned history. Replay the state of the world at a past moment and compare it to now.

02

Correlate independent evidence

Build cross-domain findings while accounting for duplicated reporting, missing observations and incomplete coverage.

03

Keep multiple explanations alive

Track competing hypotheses with explicit indicators and a revision trail showing why judgments moved.

04

Make forecasts accountable

Log predictions at a known cutoff, resolve outcomes against observed data, and score calibration against base rates.

OPTIONAL AI, NOT MANDATORY AI

Let models explain.
Let evidence decide.

Statistical detectors can identify and quantify changes. An optional hosted or local language model adds natural-language answers, briefings, and context. The same system still works without an LLM.

  • Source-backed answers and citations
  • Model-independent collection and triggers
  • Forecasts scored like any other forecaster
  • Local inference remains an option
