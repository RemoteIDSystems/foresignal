## Summary

Every record can now carry a human place context, with no online geocoder: country, first-level
region, US county, sea area and the nearest town ("12 km NNE of Austin, TX"). Tags live in their own
table beside the record, so they never create versions or change hashes. All seven tasks landed. The
four 040 operations are `implemented` and off `pending-operations.txt`.

**1. Bundled country table** (`ai.foresignal.reference`)
- `src/main/resources/reference/countries.csv` (239 countries) is generated by
  `scripts/build-countries.py` from Natural Earth 10m admin-0 v5.1.2 and the GeoNames `countryInfo.txt`
  names. The script uses only the Python standard library. The CSV's first line records both inputs'
  SHA-256.
  - Columns: `iso2` (`ISO_A2_EH`; features with `-99` are left out), `iso3`, `isoNumeric`, `fips`
    (`FIPS_10`, else GeoNames), `name`, `nameLong`, `continent`, `labelLon`/`labelLat` (`LABEL_X`/`LABEL_Y`)
    and `aliases`.
  - Aliases are the NE names, the GeoNames name and common variants: Türkiye/Turkey, Czechia/Czech
    Republic, Côte d'Ivoire/Ivory Coast, DRC and others.
- `CountryCentroids` (bean) offers `byIso2`, `byIso3`, `byFips`, `byName` (case-, accent- and
  punctuation-insensitive, aliases included) and `all()`. It needs no database and no network.
- `BundledCountryPolygons`: `countries-simple.geojson.gz` (766 KB). It is NE 10m, Douglas-Peucker at
  0.01°, rounded to 0.01°, and gives the offline `iso2` fallback.
- No IODA, abuse.ch, UNHCR or advisory adapter exists yet (only `usgs` and `system`), so nothing had to
  be switched over. The Adapter Guide tells 068, 076 and 090 to use `CountryCentroids` with
  `geometryApproximate: true`.

**2. Reference tables and loader**
- Entities (Hibernate DDL; GiST indexes in `SpatialIndexInitializer`):
  - `ref_country`, `ref_admin1` (plus `gn_code`), `ref_admin2` and `ref_sea`;
  - `gazetteer_place` (`geog geography(Point)`, GiST, KNN `<->`);
  - `gazetteer_admin1` and `gazetteer_country` (Deviation 2);
  - `ref_dataset` (plus `state` and `error`);
  - `record_region`.
- `reference/manifest.json`:
  - The datasets are `ne-admin0`, `ne-admin1`, `ne-admin2-us`, `ne-marine`, `geonames-cities15000`,
    `geonames-admin1` and `geonames-countryinfo`, plus the optional `geonames-cities1000` and
    `geonames-cities500`.
  - Every dataset has a URL, a SHA-256, an edition, a licence and an attribution.
  - The Natural Earth files are pinned to tag v5.1.2 (commit
    f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776). All 31 point-of-view admin-0 variants are listed under
    `ne-admin0.variants`, each with its own hash.
  - Marine Regions is not offered.
- `ReferenceDataLoader`:
  - **Download:** through the budgeted `UpstreamClient` (`forSystem`, id `foresignal-reference`,
    10/min and 100/day) into `${FORESIGNAL_DATA_DIR}/reference/`. Alternatively it reads a pre-placed file
    from `foresignal.reference.offline-dir`.
  - **Verify:** the SHA-256 must match; a mismatch is `REFERENCE_CHECKSUM_MISMATCH`, the file is
    deleted and nothing is loaded.
  - **Parse:** GeoJSON is streamed with Jackson and read with Foresignal's `GeoJsonReader` and JTS
    `GeometryFixer`. GeoNames TSV and zip files are parsed line by line.
  - **Swap:** rows go into a `ref_stage_*` table, then one transaction runs `TRUNCATE` + `INSERT … SELECT`
    (features sharing a key are unioned). `geom_simple = ST_SimplifyPreserveTopology(geom, 0.01)`.
  - **First start:** an async load runs (`auto-load`, default true; off in tests) and never blocks
    startup or readiness.
  - **Failures** go to `ref_dataset.error`, the `reference` readiness detail (always UP) and a
    `system.reference_load_failed` event.
  - **After a load** it publishes `ReferenceDataChangedEvent` (cache drop and retag) and
    `TriggerRulesChangedEvent` (presets recompile).
- API (`ReferenceController`): `GET /v1/reference`, `POST /v1/reference/load` (admin, 202, names
  only), `GET /v1/reference/countries` and `GET /v1/reference/reverse`.
- Licensing:
  - The GeoNames CC BY 4.0 credit appears in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `GET /v1/reference`,
    `RegionTags.attribution` and GeoJSON `properties.nearAttribution`.
  - `RegionAttribution` holds the credits for 109.
  - Natural Earth is credited anyway. The boundary note is in the status and the Operator Guide.

**3. RegionTagger**
- `tag(Geometry)` works on the representative point (JTS `getInteriorPoint`, the `ST_PointOnSurface`
  equivalent).
- Country, admin-1 and county use `ST_Covers` with a `&&` prefilter.
- Coastal fallback: a point within 5 km of a country gets the nearest one (by distance to the
  simplified boundary). At sea, `iso2` is null and `sea` is set.
- `near` is the KNN nearest place (population ≥ `near-min-population`, default 0). It carries the
  distance and the bearing from the place, and its `admin1` is mapped to ISO 3166-2 through
  `ref_admin1.gn_code`.
- A 50,000-entry LRU keyed on coordinates rounded to 0.01° caches results.
- Each missing table leaves its members null. Without `ref_country` the country comes from the bundled
  polygons. A database error degrades to the bundled country; tagging never fails.

**4. `record_region`**
- Key `(source_id, external_id)`, with indexes `(iso2)` and `(iso2, admin1)`. It is written only by
  `RegionTagStore`, in transactions of its own, and is part of no hash.
- `RegionTagWorker`:
  - It keeps the durable cursor `region.cursor` over ChangeBus and joins the consumer clamp
    (`RegionCursorConsumer`). It also subscribes to LiveBus.
  - It retags only past `retag-km` (2 km, or 10 km for TRACK). A tombstone deletes the row.
  - It skips empty seq gaps in one step.
  - It retags every current eager record after a reference load and on the first start.
- `regionTagging: eager|lazy|off` per source:
  - It is stored in `app_setting['regionTagging']` (038's precedent) and exposed as
    `SourceSettings.regionTagging`.
  - The default is eager for STANDARD and lazy for TRACK.
  - Lazy tagging (`RegionTagger.tagRecord`) happens when an alert fires (and its template renders) and
    on `include=region`.
- Rows are removed by the orphan sweep (`RegionRetentionStep`, order 460) and by 039's purge fan-out,
  which now finds the table.
- Alerts snapshot `alert.region` (jsonb) at fire time.

**5. Templates, filters, presets**
- Placeholders: `{{near.name|km|mi|bearing|text}}`, `{{near.text|imperial}}`, `{{country}}`,
  `{{country.name}}`, `{{admin1}}`, `{{admin1.name}}`, `{{county}}` and `{{sea}}`.
  - Notifications read the alert's snapshot.
  - A channel's `config.units: imperial` switches `{{near.text}}` to miles (Deviation 8).
- Filters `country`, `admin1` and `county` work on:
  - records, both list and GeoJSON (streaming and count included);
  - `/v1/search` (record documents through `record_region`, alert documents through the snapshot);
  - `/v1/alerts`, live stream matching included;
  - the three stats reads (raw rows);
  - `BriefingScope` (digest SQL, and LLM context through the narrowed restriction).
  Unknown codes are `400 UNKNOWN_REGION`.
- `geofenceRef` (`GeofenceRef`, stored in `trigger_rule.geofence_ref`):
  - `RuleSqlBuilder` renders `ST_Intersects(x.geom, (SELECT ST_Multi(ST_CollectionExtract(ST_Union(u.g),
    3)) FROM (… ref_country/ref_admin1/ref_admin2 … ) u))`.
  - `RuleSpecs` resolves the same union through `RegionFences`/`RegionGeometries` into the fence the
    `TriggerPersistGate` prepares.
  - Saving a preset without its reference data is `409 REFERENCE_DATA_MISSING`.
  - The truth table gains `geofenceRefRegions`/`geofenceRefRows`.

**6. Embeddings, MCP, records**

## Deviations from the prompt

1. **GeoNames checksums.** GeoNames regenerates its dumps daily: `countryInfo.txt` was last modified
   2026-10-12 02:17 UTC, the day it was fetched. A pinned hash would fail within a day, so the
   GeoNames entries are marked `rolling`.
   - Their manifest hashes (recorded 2026-10-12) are verified only when the operator pins one with
     `foresignal.reference.checksums.<name>`. Otherwise the observed SHA-256 is recorded in
     `ref_dataset`.
   - The Natural Earth files are strictly verified.
2. **Extra tables and columns:**
   - `gazetteer_admin1` and `gazetteer_country` hold the `geonames-admin1` and `geonames-countryinfo`
     datasets, which the prompt names but gives no table.
   - `ref_admin1.gn_code` (NE `gn_a1_code`) maps a GeoNames place to its ISO 3166-2 region.
   - `ref_dataset.state` and `ref_dataset.error` back the spec's `ReferenceDataset.state` and
     `ReferenceDataset.error`.
3. **Downloads without a registered source:** `UpstreamClient.forSystem` plus
   `BudgetGovernor.fixBudget` give the loader the same budget, backoff, metering and User-Agent path
   as an adapter. The id is `foresignal-reference`, which is not in `/v1/sources`.
4. **Region filters travel on `ReadRestriction.regionFilter`.** It is request-scoped and never
   stored, and it is part of the canonical form (cursors, ETags). The paths that implement the filters
   add `RegionFilter.sql` themselves, because the predicate needs the external id that the
   `RecordFilter` SQL API does not have. The filters cannot be combined with `asOf` (400) and are not
   applied to `/v1/history`, which the spec does not list.
5. **Stats with a region filter always read raw rows.** The rollup has no regions until 119, and
   `by=cell` with a region filter is refused.
6. **Geofence presets:**
   - The union is resolved when the rule is compiled and is never stored in `trigger_rule.geofence`.
   - Rules recompile after every reference load.
   - `hysteresisMeters` with `geofenceRef` is a 400; growing or shrinking a country polygon on the
     ellipsoid is not offered.
   - `geofenceRef`, `geofence` and `near` are mutually exclusive.
   - Missing reference data at evaluation marks the rule broken, as an unknown operator does.
7. **Lazy tagging when a briefing is built is not done.** Briefing statistics read stored tags, so a
   lazy source matches a briefing's region scope only after its records were tagged by an alert or a
   read. Lazy tagging happens on alert fire (and template rendering) and on `include=region`, capped
   at 200 records per page; streaming formats do not add `region`.
8. **Channel units:** 018 never built a `units` setting, so `config.units` (`metric` | `imperial`,
   validated) was added to the channel config and the spec.
9. **Text rules:**
   - The suffix after a place's name is the state code in the US ("Austin, TX") and the country name
     elsewhere ("Monterrey, Mexico"), because GeoNames admin-1 codes outside the US are numeric.
   - Within one unit of the place the text reads "in Austin, TX". Distances are whole numbers.
   - `{{county}}` renders the county name, or its FIPS5 when unnamed.
10. **Cache rounding.** Tags are computed for the point rounded to 0.01° (the cache key);
    `tagged_lon`/`tagged_lat` keep the exact point for the retag distance.
11. **Extra fields:** GeoJSON features with `near` also gain `properties.nearAttribution`, the
    GeoNames credit.
12. **Spec reconciliations** (changelog entry 040):
    - the four operations are implemented;
    - the 400 descriptions gain `UNKNOWN_REGION`;
    - `listAlerts` gains a 400;
    - `createTrigger` and `updateTrigger` gain 409 `REFERENCE_DATA_MISSING`;
    - the `messageTemplate` description lists the region placeholders;
    - `ChannelInput` and `Channel` config gain `units`;
    - `Problem` lists `UNKNOWN_REGION` (400) and `REFERENCE_DATA_MISSING` (409);
    - `REFERENCE_CHECKSUM_MISMATCH` is documented as reported in `ReferenceDataset.error`, never as an
      HTTP status.
13. **Test-support changes:**
    - `RetentionItSupport.awaitTriggerCursor` and `RecordLifecycleIT` also wait for the region
      worker's cursor, which now joins the clamp.
    - `EntityPurgeIT`'s region test no longer creates and drops its own `record_region`, because
      Hibernate owns the table now.
    - `StatsControllerIT` expects `country=` to work (`region=` is still refused for 119).
