## Summary

Five new conflict and civil-alert sources. All are **disabled by default**, STANDARD tier, policy ALL, with no record
TTL (events end by tombstone) and 1825 d retention (`event` class). All five pass `DescriptorValidator` and
`IngestPolicyValidator`, checked with a scratch `main` (see "Compile result"). The keyed sources need their
credentials before they can be enabled: `alerts-in-ua` (`token`), `acled` (`email`, `password`) and `ucdp-ged`
(`token`).

**Egress and license hooks (task 1):**
- Prompt 062's `Egress` / `EgressPolicy` already exist; nothing was added there.
- `acled` is `egress: LOCAL_ONLY`. The other four sources are `OPEN`. None is `egressOverridable`.
- The free-text `licence`, `termsUrl` and `attribution` values are set as prompted, with the terms findings recorded
  below.

**Ukraine boundaries (task 2):**
- **Bundled data** (`src/main/resources/geo/ukr/`): `adm1.geojson` (27 units) and `adm2.geojson` (139 units: the
  2020 raions, Kyiv, Sevastopol and the Chornobyl zone).
  - Source: geoBoundaries **gbHumanitarian** UKR, release commit `9469f09`. See Deviation 1.
  - Each polygon is simplified to at most 1,500 vertices; the largest has 1,479. The two files total 1.70 MB.
  - The version and attribution are recorded in `geo/ukr/NOTICE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- **Crosswalks:**
  - `alerts-in-ua-uids.json`: 1,622 UIDs from the alerts.in.ua location sheet linked from the developer docs.
  - `siren-region-ids.json`: 1,606 ids from `siren.pp.ua/api/v3/regions`.
  - Both are generated by the new `scripts/build-ukr-regions.py` (standard library only). It matches names through
    the 2010 national romanization plus a six-entry table of raions renamed in 2024 (and one typo in the source).
  - Every oblast, city with special status and raion resolves. Every hromada or community resolves to its raion.
  - Only siren region `0` ("Тестовий регіон", the wrapper's test region) is unmatched.
- **`UaRegionIndex`** (`ai.foresignal.reference.ukr`, a `@Component` shared by the two alert adapters):
  - `byAlertsUid` and `bySirenRegionId` return the exact polygon, or the raion with `approximate`.
  - An unknown id is logged once per table and id, and yields no geometry.
  - Files are loaded lazily on the first lookup.
  - Polygons that simplification and rounding left invalid (22 of 166, for example a ring touching itself) are
    repaired with JTS `GeometryFixer` on load; all 166 are then valid.

**`alerts-in-ua`** (`AlertsInUaAdapter`, `AlertsInUaParser`):
- **Request:** a conditional GET with `Authorization: Bearer` (never a query parameter). The stored `Last-Modified`
  goes back as `If-Modified-Since`; a 304 means unchanged.
- **Errors:** 401 → `CredentialsRejectedException`. A 429 backs off in `UpstreamClient`.
- **Records:** kinds `air-raid-alert` and `threat-alert`; `externalId = id`.
  - Payload: `location, locationEn, locationType, oblast, raion, alertType, alertLevel, threatTypes` (sorted,
    distinct), `notes` (≤ 1 KB, untrusted) and `geometryApproximate`. The threats' `source_message` is dropped.
  - `observedAt = updated_at`, `eventTime = started_at`.
- **End of an alert:** `finished_at` → tombstone at once. Absence from two complete lists → `AbsenceTombstoner`
  tombstone, scoped per kind.
- **Descriptor:** 30 s (min 20 s), budget 3/min and 4,320/day. The description carries the upstream disclaimer.

**`ua-air-raid-siren`** (`UaSirenAdapter`, `UaSirenParser`):
- **Request:** a conditional GET; the upstream sends an `ETag`.
- **Records:** one per region and type (`externalId = regionId:type`). A region also lists its parent's alerts under
  the parent's `regionId`, so such an alert is kept once, newest `lastUpdate` first.
  - Payload: `region, regionEn, regionType, alertType, alertLevel` (the highest, lower case) and
    `geometryApproximate`.
  - `observedAt = lastUpdate`, `eventTime` = the earliest `createdAt`.
- **Absence:** two misses → tombstone.
- **Descriptor:** 30 s (min 15 s), budget 4/min and 4,000/day. License, description and status as prompted.

**`deepstate-frontline`** (`DeepStateAdapter`, `DeepStateParser`, `GeodesicArea`):
- **Fetching:** today's UTC file is tried first, then yesterday's on a 404, each conditional per file name. The
  validators of older names are forgotten.
- **Pinning:** with a 40-hex `ref`, `fileDate` is required. The file is fetched once and is not-modified afterwards
  with no request. `fileDate` with a branch ref reads that day only.
- **Record:** the single entity `occupied-area`; the geometry is kept as published and simplified only above 9,500
  vertices.
  - Payload: `mapDate`, `areaKm2`, `areaChangeKm2` and `ref`.
  - `areaKm2` is the WGS84 ellipsoidal area via the authalic latitude: 12,308.5 km² for a 1° cell at the equator,
    against 12,308.8 for the exact value. The real maps give 117,147.4 and 117,154.9 km².
  - `areaChangeKm2` compares with the previous map date, kept in state.
  - `observedAt = eventTime` = the map date at 03:00 UTC.
- **Descriptor:** 6 h (min 1 h), 20/day.

**`acled`** (`AcledAdapter`, `AcledParser`, `AcledTokens`):
- **Tokens:**
  - The password grant and the refresh grant (5 minutes before expiry) are as the guide says.
  - A refused refresh falls back to a new login. A changed email or password invalidates the cache (it is keyed by a
    SHA-256 fingerprint).
  - Tokens are held in memory only. A restart logs in again.
- **Events:** `iso` comes from param `countries` (ISO 3166 numeric, required, ≤ 30).
  - The first run reads an `event_date` window of `lookbackDays` (default 30); later runs read
    `timestamp>={cursor}&timestamp_where=>=`.
  - Pages are read until one holds fewer than 5,000 rows (at most 20). The cursor advances only after every page
    succeeded.
- **Deletions:** `/api/deleted/read?deleted_timestamp={cursor}` (Deviation 4). Ids of the configured countries,
  matched by the ISO alpha-3 prefix of `event_id_cnty`, become tombstones.
- **Retries:** a 401 on data refreshes the token once and retries.
- **Refusals:** a 403 after login is a refusal with health reason "account access level does not allow this query"
  (`accessDeniedIsTerminal` plus `accessDeniedReasons`: one-hour backoff, nothing re-sent). The consent and
  incomplete-profile 403 texts get reasons too.
- **Records:** `quality.accuracyMeters` follows the prompted `geo_precision` mapping.
  - Payload as prompted; `notes` is ≤ 2 KB and the embed field, `sources` is ≤ 1 KB.
  - `observedAt = timestamp`, `eventTime = event_date`.

**`ucdp-ged`** (`UcdpGedAdapter`, `UcdpParser`):
- **Auth:** `x-ucdp-access-token`, redacted like every credential value. 401 → `CredentialsRejectedException`.
- **Version discovery:** daily, cached in state. It probes `YY.0.M` backwards across the year boundary, up to 14
  probes. A probe that errors (other than a 429 or an I/O failure) counts as empty. Param `version` overrides
  discovery.
- **Events:** `StartDate`/`EndDate`, `pagesize=1000`.
  - Paging takes the next page number from the first page's `NextPageUrl` and iterates to `TotalPages` (at most 25),
    so no page number is guessed. See Deviation 6.
  - `datasetVersion` is volatile.
- **Windowed absence:** a new `AbsenceTombstoner.update(…, forget)` overload drops ids whose `date_end` left the
  window, without tombstoning them. An event missing twice inside the window is tombstoned. The `date_end` of each
  tracked id is kept in state (`ucdp:ends`, gzipped above 32 KB).
- **`where_prec`:** mapped per the GED codebook 26.1. See Deviation 5.
- **Time:** `observedAtMode` RECEIVED.
- **Params:** `includeAnnual` is reserved (default false) and logged if set.

**Shared:**
- `adapters.conflict.ConflictSources` holds the test base-URL property, JSON, text and time helpers, state packing
  and field-spec helpers.
- **Framework hardening (SRC-5):** `UrlRedactor.redactTokenMembers` masks the `access_token`, `refresh_token` and
  `id_token` values of recorded response bodies. `BoundSourceHttp.recordExchange` applies it, so a recorded ACLED (or
  OpenSky) token response never stores a run-time token.

## Deviations from the prompt

1. **Boundaries come from geoBoundaries gbHumanitarian, not gbOpen, and are CC BY 3.0 IGO, not CC BY 4.0.**
   - Verified 2026-10-13 via `geoboundaries.org/api/current/…/UKR/…`:
     - gbOpen UKR ADM1 is OpenStreetMap data under **ODbL 1.0**;
     - gbOpen ADM2 is the **2006 pre-reform raions** (495 units, Wikimedia, public domain), which do not match the
       alerts' current raions.
   - gbHumanitarian (State Scientific Production Enterprise "Kartographia" via OCHA HDX, 2022) has the 27 oblast-level
     units and the 139 current raion-level units, under **CC BY 3.0 IGO**.
   - That license is recorded in the NOTICE, the third-party notices and the catalog.
2. **Size budget.** The 1,500-vertex cap and ≤ 2 MB apply to the boundary files: 1.70 MB together. The two crosswalks
   add 0.46 MB, for 2.16 MB in `geo/ukr/` in all.
3. **Budgets raised where one run needs more requests than the prompted per-minute budget allows.** `BudgetGovernor`
   refuses rather than queues, and its per-minute bucket has a capacity of the per-minute value.
   - `deepstate-frontline`: 2/min instead of 1/min. Today's 404 plus yesterday's file is 2 requests between 00:00
     and about 03:00 UTC.
   - `acled`: 30/min instead of 2/min. A token request plus pages plus deletions.
   - `ucdp-ged`: 30/min instead of 2/min. Up to 14 probes plus pages.
   - The daily budgets are as prompted (20, 200, 300). UCDP's real limit is per day (5,000).
4. **ACLED deletions filter on `deleted_timestamp`.** The deleted endpoint guide (read 2026-10-13) lists only
   `event_id_cnty` and `deleted_timestamp` (default type `>=`), not `timestamp`.
   - The first run starts the deletion cursor at `now − lookbackDays`.
   - The endpoint has no country filter, so deletions are kept only for the configured countries, by the ISO
     alpha-3 prefix of `event_id_cnty`. The numeric codes are mapped through the bundled `CountryCentroids` table.
5. **UCDP `where_prec`.** The prompt says 7 is the country-level code. The GED codebook 26.1 (`ged261.pdf`, read
   2026-10-13) says:
   - 6 = "only the country where the event took place is known";
   - 7 = "event in international waters or airspace".

   So 6 uses the country's label point from `CountryCentroids` (the name, else the name before a parenthesis, else
   the coded point), and 7 keeps the coded point. Both get `geometryApproximate` and no accuracy. Codes 3, 4 and 5
   (ADM2, ADM1, linear or fuzzy) are 50, 150 and 250 km; the prompt left them unspecified ("…").
6. **UCDP paging.** The docs' example (a request without `page` answering `PreviousPageUrl …page=0` and
   `NextPageUrl …page=2`) leaves the first page index ambiguous.
   - The adapter requests the first page without `page` and takes the index of the next one from `NextPageUrl`.
     Only the integer is read; the URL is never followed.
   - With no `NextPageUrl`, it assumes 0-based.
7. **Unknown alerts.in.ua UID → no geometry,** as prompted. There is no fallback to `location_oblast_uid`.
8. **`geometryApproximate` on `ua-air-raid-siren`.** Community alerts carry it, as `alerts-in-ua` does for
   hromadas, so the OpenAPI payloadFields were amended (with a changelog entry).
9. **Raion matching** needed a table of renames, because the 2022 boundary data predates the 2024 renamings:
   - Sheptytskyi → Chervonohradskyi;
   - Zviahelskyi → Novohrad-Volynskyi;
   - Volodymyrskyi → Volodymyr-Volynskyi;
   - Samarivskyi → Novomoskovskyi;
   - Berestynskyi → Krasnohradskyi;
   - Siverskodonetskyi → Sievierodonetskyi;
   - plus the source's own typo "Cnernivetskyi".

   The boundary ids keep the data's names, for example `adm2:lvivska:chervonohradskyi`.
10. **Base-URL test property:** one `foresignal.test.adapters.conflict.base-url`, rebasing as `/{host}{path}` (the
    072–079 scheme).

   checkpoint.
