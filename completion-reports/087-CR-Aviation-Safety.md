## Summary

The US aviation-safety picture: TFRs with XNOTAM polygons and validity, NAS airport delays, SIGMETs/AIRMETs,
G-AIRMETs, CWAs, PIREPs and TAFs from AWC, the FAA UAS Facility Map and six FAA UAS restriction layers. All sources
are disabled by default, licensed `US-PD`, and use the budgeted upstream client.

**1. `faa-tfr`** (`adapters.faa.FaaTfrAdapter`, `XnotamParser`, `FaaXml`):
- **List:** `exportTfrList` (verified 2026-10-14: 76 TFRs, `no-cache`, no validators). Each row is hashed (SHA-256
  over its fields).
- **Details:** fetched only for new rows, changed rows and details older than 24 h. At most 40 per run, serial. A
  404/410 or unreadable detail is retried on a later run.
- **State:** `tfr:rows` = `{id: {h, t}}`.
- **Tombstones:** a row that leaves the list is tombstoned at once.
- **`XnotamParser`:**
  - uses the hardened StAX factory (a DOCTYPE is refused);
  - geometry: each `TFRAreaGroup`'s `abdMergedArea`, else its `Abd` parts. GRC/RHL vertices are points; `CWA`/`CCA`
    arcs run from the previous to the next vertex, densified on the sphere in steps of at most 2°; a lone `CIR` is a
    64-vertex circle (NM/KM/M/FT radii); several areas form a MultiPolygon;
  - vertical limits: the lowest floor and the highest ceiling over all `aseTFRArea`s (FL × 100), with their
    SFC/ALT/HEI codes;
  - text: `txtDescrTraditional`, else `txtDescrModern` with the markup stripped, capped at 8,000 characters.
- **Record:**
  - externalId = the list's `notam_id`;
  - `observedAt` = `dateIssued`, `validFrom` = `dateEffective`, `validUntil` = `dateExpire` (null means until
    further notice);
  - the prompt's payload, title, tile, search and embed fields;
  - 15 min, 45/min, 2,000/day, retention 1825 d.

**2. `faa-nas-status`** (`FaaNasStatusAdapter`, `NasStatusParser`, `UsAirports`):
- **Format:** XML (the catalog's "REST JSON" is corrected).
- **Parsing:** every `Delay_type` entry that has an `ARPT` child. Kinds are `ground-delay`, `ground-stop`,
  `arrival-delay`/`departure-delay` (from `Type`), `closure` and `deicing`; any other block name becomes kebab case.
  Durations are converted to minutes.
- **`reopen`:** taken from the closure NOTAM's own validity (`…2605271826-2705281600`), else from the `Reopen` text,
  moved to the next year when that date would already be past.
- **Geometry:** from `src/main/resources/reference/us-airports.csv` (14,090 airports, generated by
  `scripts/build-us-airports.py` from OurAirports' public-domain data). An unknown airport has no geometry and is
  counted.
- **Removal:** `LiveIdSet` tombstones a program once it is cleared.
- **Schedule:** 5 min, 2/min, 600/day.

- **`awc-cwas`:** 10 min, 1/min, 500/day.
- **Shared by these three:** the `aviation-hazard` payload. An advisory missing from the next complete response is

## Deviations from the prompt

1. **XNOTAM times are always UTC.** The prompt says "UTC unless `codeTimeZone` says otherwise; convert". Every TFR
   whose text quotes both says otherwise. For example 6/9370 has `codeTimeZone` EDT and `dateEffective`
   2026-10-16T20:30:00, and its text reads "EFFECTIVE 2610162030 UTC (1630 LOCAL)". So values are read as UTC, and an
   explicit offset is honored. `codeTimeZone` is only the local zone the text quotes.
2. **Vertical limits** are not on the area geometry but on each `aseTFRArea`. The payload's single
   `lowerFt`/`upperFt` are the lowest floor and highest ceiling over all areas.
3. **TFR id from the list, not `txtLocalName`:** 6/8928's `txtLocalName` is "261014 Atlanta, GA VIP".
4. **The TFR "without expiry" fixture is real (4/3634).** A hand-made one with arcs exists too; 14 of the 76 live
   TFRs have no `dateExpire`.
5. **TFR details are re-fetched every 24 h** even when the row is unchanged, and unchanged rows emit no records. This
   recovers a record lost downstream and stays within budget; the prompt only says "only for ids that are new or
   whose list row hash changed". A run that emitted nothing returns `unchanged`. A run cut by the 40-fetch cap or a
   failed detail is partial (`complete=false`).
6. **`faa-uasfm` incremental query.** `LAST_EDIT` is a text field (`"5/8/2017"`), so `LAST_EDIT > {lastMax}` would
   compare text. In a quiet box the newest `LAST_EDIT` can also be years old (2022-11-15 for Austin). Incremental
   runs therefore ask `LAST_EDIT IN ('M/d/yyyy', …)` for every day from 7 days before the last complete read through
   today; the text match was verified on the layer. The 56-day full reload stays as the backstop.
7. **The `faa-uas-*` ids keep the prompt's names.** 073's validator only accepted `feature-…`, so its built-in pattern
   now also admits the reviewed prefix `faa-uas-` (the OpenAPI already listed these ids).
8. **`faa-uas-*` field mapping:**
   - `name` is `Base` (with `Facility` as `facility`) on the national security layers;
   - `city` (and the id fields `objectId`/`globalId`) are added where the layers have them;
   - `uas-site` has no `floorFt`/`reason` (the layer has none);
   - the 57 "2,000' MSL" ceilings carry no `ceilingFt` (the AGL texts are value-mapped);
   - ids: `OBJECTID` for the two national security layers (no GlobalID; `FAA_ID` repeats for 10 rows) and
     `GLOBALID`/`GLOBAL_ID` elsewhere. OBJECTID may change if the FAA republishes the layer.
9. **G-AIRMET id:** `:level` is appended for freezing-level contours. One tag carries every contour (7 FZLVL features
   at five levels in the capture), and pieces of one contour are merged into one record with a multi-part geometry.
   The prompt's id alone would have dropped four contours.
10. **No hand-made "forecast > 0" G-AIRMET:** every live snapshot had `forecast` 3.
11. **CWA fixture is synthetic** (from AWC's OpenAPI): none was in force at capture.
12. **NAS fixture:** the types that were not live (ground delay, ground stop, deicing) are synthetic, with the
    prompt's element names. Unknown block names are handled generically.
13. **AWC hazards are tombstoned when they leave the feed** (`LiveIdSet`), so a canceled SIGMET or CWA stops reading
    as current before `validTimeTo`. The prompt names no removal rule for them.
14. **`aviation-hazard` payload:** `severity` is text (a number for US SIGMETs, LGT/MOD/SEV for G-AIRMETs).
    `movementDir` is degrees: international compass points are converted, and a freezing-level contour's level is
    its `baseFt`. **`pirep`** gains `reportType` (PIREP / Urgent PIREP / AIREP; urgency is worth keeping); the spec
    is amended.
15. **PIREP box order:** AWC's `bbox` is `lat0,lon0,lat1,lon1` (OpenAPI and live check). The param stays Foresignal's
    west,south,east,north with the prompt's default and is converted. The live PIREP fields differ from AWC's
    OpenAPI: `obsTime` is ISO text, and the properties are `fltlvl` and `airepType`.
16. **Retention classes:**
    - TFRs, delays and hazards: `event`;
    - PIREPs: `bulk-event` (90 d);
    - TAFs: 7 d like METARs (FamilyDefaultsTest's weather table gains `taf`);
    - the UAS grid and restriction layers: `reference` (90 d).
    The prompt names none.
17. **"Not active until it starts"** (AviationIngestIT) is asserted through a `forecastHorizon = 0` rule's
    `RuleSqlBuilder` statement, which is where 008's `validFrom` semantics live. Current reads (`/v1/records`) do not
    filter on `validFrom`.

## Issues found / open questions

1. **Owner question (TAF validity):** a TAF is usually issued 30–60 minutes before its validity starts, so with
   `validFrom` = valid-from (as the prompt says) each station's newest TAF reads "not yet valid" for that time, while
   the previous TAF is already replaced. Should `validFrom` be left null for TAFs, or should the previous TAF stay
   current until the new one starts?
2. **Owner question (G-AIRMET timing):** a 3-hour snapshot has `validFrom` = `validTime` (issue + 3 h). With the
   3-hour TTL on receipt it is current from `validTime` until 3 h after it was last seen. Confirm this is the wanted
   reading of "`validFrom` = `validTime`; `recordTtl` 3 h".
3. **Owner question (retention):** confirm `bulk-event` for PIREPs, 7 d for TAFs and `reference` for the UAS layers,
   or name classes for them in the Conventions table.
4. **Owner question (validator):** the reviewed-prefix allowance in `FeatureSpecValidator.ID_PATTERN` is a list of
   one (`faa-uas`). Should kit specs keep catalog ids like this, or should built-in kit ids always be `feature-…`?
5. **Unverified at capture (143):**
   - NAS ground delay, ground stop and deicing entries;
   - a live CWA;
   - a busy-day PIREP count against the 400-entry cap;
   - whether the FAA republishes `DoD_Mar_13` with new OBJECTIDs.
6. **US English:** all new text is US English (the workspace guard passed). The descriptor builder method
   `licence(...)` is an existing API and was not renamed.
7. **Instructions in data:** none. The TFR list and details, the NAS document, AWC's API page and OpenAPI, the ArcGIS
   service directory and the OurAirports data were read; none addressed an agent.
